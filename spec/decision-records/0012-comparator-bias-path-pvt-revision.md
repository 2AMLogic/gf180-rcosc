- **Status**: Ratified — records simulation evidence. Does **not** supersede any
  row of [0002](0002-target-spec-ratification.md)/[0003](0003-pdk-sourced-process-spread-tcr-and-iq.md);
  does not reopen [0004](0004-no-active-tc-compensation-runtime-discipline.md)'s

  record (issue #39).
- **Date**: 2026-09-21
- **Decided by**: Builder agent, issue #39

## Context

DR-0006 isolated the dominant residual of the post-resize PVT campaign: once
trim saturation was removed, the comparator/bias path's own supply and
temperature sensitivity — never budgeted by DR-0003's timing-TCR-only post-trim
derivation — was the main error left standing (DR-0006, and the 20260907T090653Z
campaign: post-trim supply sensitivity `ss` +20.77% / `tt` +8.52% at
per-corner calibrated codes; ΔT coefficients +856…+1923 ppm/K against the
budgeted ≈ +1177 ppm/K). DR-0006 said the follow-up that wants to close the gap
"should target that sensitivity specifically … e.g. the bias generator's
supply/temperature dependence, not further timing-R/C retuning," and DR-0004
itself pre-authorized exactly this correction path: "if the ratiometric bias
generator's own supply/PVT sensitivity turns out to dominate over the
timing-resistor TCR this record's arithmetic assumed — that must be corrected
via a further superseding decision record." Issue #39 is that follow-up.

Issue #39's instrumentation (node-crossing timestamps via the
`design/netlist/pvt_tb.spice` deck, issue #16's method) measured, before any
change:

- The current reference was `ibias = (VDD − V_GS(MBIASD))/RBIAS` — a VDD-linear
  numerator carrying **+24.5% supply sensitivity over 3.0→3.6 V** at the
  reference corner, ~+900 ppm/K of its own, and nominally ±0% at the
  ratiometric-ideal the post-trim arithmetic implicitly assumed.
- The comparator delay is paid **≈3× into the charge phase**: the timing node
  `vc` keeps charging while the comparator/latch respond, so the threshold
  overshoot is `slope × t_delay` and the period pays `~3 × t_delay` for every
  nanosecond of comparator+latch latency (`design/README.md` issue #16 writeup;
  re-measured here at ≈1.7–2.9 ns comparator + 0.3–0.7 ns latch per crossing,
  ≈25–30% of the period at slow corners).
- The per-corner ΔT spread (`fs` +1923 ppm/K vs `sf` +856 ppm/K, at identical
  passive corners) is the signature of that device-driven delay term: it
  disappears with the current reference stabilized (below).

A second, distinct, **out-of-scope** defect was isolated by the same
instrumentation and is **not** addressed by this record: the trim-bank pass
switches (`SW0..SW7`) lose effectiveness across the top of the charge ramp,
because each switch's effective `V_GS = t_i − v(c_{i+1})` collapses as the
timing node rises into the body-effected threshold region. The realized trim
curve shows the symptom directly: bit-mid codes are near-dead
(`0x00`→`0x10` +0.04%) while MSB-boundary codes jump (`0x70`→`0x80` +33%),
and the high-R codes carry a "phantom" series resistance that is itself
PVT-dependent (`ss`@`0xEF` supply sensitivity +20.0% vs `ss`@`0x80` +11.0%,
pre-revision). This mechanism — a *switch* design flaw, not a resistor-value
question — is filed as its own follow-up issue (trim-bank pass-switch
strengthening) rather than folded into this one; one issue, one mechanism
(CLAUDE.md decomposition discipline).

## Decision

**The bias generator's current reference is re-architected to a self-biased
(supply-independent) beta-multiplier core. No ratified spec figure is
superseded, and no compensation of the timing resistor's TCR is attempted
(DR-0004 stands).**

`design/rcosc_bias.sch` changes (issue #39):

- `RBIAS`/`MBIASD` (the `ibias = (VDD − V_GS)/R` leg) are **removed**,
  together with their VDD-linear numerator and its +24.5% supply slope.
- A self-biased core replaces them: pfet mirror pair `P1` (diode, branch 2) /
  `P2` (mirror, branch 1), `N1` (`W=2u L=1u`, diode-connected) as the
  reference unit — **its gate node is the exported `ibias` net, so
  `rcosc_comparator.sch`'s `MTAIL` (`W=16u nf=8`) mirrors it 8:1 exactly as it
  mirrored the old `MBIASD`, with zero comparator-side changes** — and `N2`
  (`W=16u nf=8`, gate on the `ibias` node, source degenerated by `RZ`,
  `ppolyf_u_1k W=2u L=32u`) closing the loop. The loop equates the `N1`/`N2`
  `V_GS` difference across `RZ`, and **VDD appears nowhere in that
  constraint**.
- `SEED` (`nfet_03v3 W=2u L=20u`, gate at `vl`, drain on the PMOS gate bus,
  source `vss`) is a weak always-on pull-down (~1 µA) that makes the loop's
  zero-current state non-stationary. Its gate draws no DC current from the
  `vl` divider node, so the ratiometric thresholds are unperturbed. Startup
  was verified in the full-oscillator transient at the reference corner
  (`design/netlist/smoke_test.log`): the oscillator free-runs from the
  circuit's all-zero initial state within the first few periods, measured
  edges 5..25 well inside the `tran 200p 1200n` window.
- `RBA`/`RBB`/`RBC` and the vh/vl ratiometric behavior are **unchanged**. The
  trim bank (`rcosc_trim_bank.sch`) is **untouched** — per this issue's
  explicit guardrail, R values were not re-tuned.

The core restores the assumption DR-0003's post-trim arithmetic was built on —
a nominally fixed current/voltage reference for the comparator path — by
making the reference current supply-independent **as measured:** reference
supply sensitivity falls from +24.5% (pre-#39, instrumented) to a few percent
loop residue, and the per-corner ΔT spread collapses from
+856…+1923 ppm/K (DR-0009, calibrated codes) to **+774…+1003 ppm/K** across all
seven corners at their per-corner calibrated codes (full campaign,
`20260921T075822Z`) — the pure-TCR-class band the post-trim full-temperature
row's +8%/−9% arithmetic budgets. The full-temperature row's upper excess
correspondingly improves from +27.85% to +13.03%. The residual supply
sensitivity at the slow-corner calibrated codes (`ss` +20.05%, `sf` +12.02%,
`rc_s` +13.70% over 3.0→3.6 V) is dominated by the trim-bank phantom path
named above, which this issue deliberately does not touch.

**The scale of the accuracy miss shrinks materially but the two post-trim
rows are still not met**, for the same honest reasons the instrumentation
predicts: the residual per-cell error that remains is now dominated by (a) the
trim-bank pass-switch "phantom resistance" path — its own supply sensitivity
is visible per-code (`ss`@0xEF vs `ss`@0x80 above) and is a separate
mechanism filed separately, and (b) the comparator/latch delay residue. No
ratified row is relaxed, and no result is rounded up to "met": the verdicts
come from the simulated numbers, per the record discipline DR-0005/0006 set.

the post-#39 schematic until that re-verification lands.

## Alternatives considered

- **Retune `RBIAS` further (the #16/#22/#24 lever).** Rejected: it re-sizes
  the same `(VDD − V_GS)/R` numerator and cannot remove the VDD-linear supply
  term DR-0006 names; DR-0009 already showed the lever buys trim range at Iq
  cost, not accuracy.
- **Bandgap or TC-canceling reference (BLATANTLY reopening DR-0004).**
  Rejected: DR-0004 is a ratified record and its evidence (no PDK-published
  positive-TCR device; no published V_Vbe data for a parasitic BJT; closed-loop
  compensation is real design scope) is unchanged by this issue. A flat
  *supply-independent* current that the comparator path's *unbudgeted*
  overhead was implicitly assumed to run on is not TC compensation of the
  timing resistor; anything that would cross into TC cancellation (e.g. shaping
  the current reference so the free-running tempco lands meaningfully below
  the budgeted +8%/−9%) still requires its own superseding record with new
  evidence. This revision deliberately stops short of that.
- **Fix the trim-bank pass switches in the same PR.** Rejected:
  one-issue-one-mechanism discipline. The phantom-resistance mechanism is
  real, instrumented, quantified above, and filed as its own follow-up issue
  (#43);
  mixing it here would make the accuracy-row attribution un-auditable.
- **Do nothing and record the DR-0006 residual as permanent.** Rejected:
  DR-0006 explicitly warranted this targeted revision attempt first.

## Consequences

- `README.md`'s target-spec table is **unchanged** by this record.
- `design/rcosc_bias.sch` carries the re-architected reference; the comparator,
  trim bank, and top-level interface nets (`ibias` remains a gate-voltage net
  driving `MTAIL`) are untouched.
- The post-trim accuracy rows remain **not met**, but the dominant
  unbudgeted mechanism DR-0006 isolated is now measured, removed where it was
  removable, and bounded; the remaining error is attributed to two named
  mechanisms: the trim-bank pass-switch phantom path (follow-up issue) and
  the comparator/latch delay residue.
- `design/README.md` "Bias generator" and "Full PVT-corner re-verification"
  sections now cite this record and the new campaign;
  `sim/README.md`'s campaign index gains the new run-id rows (PVT and Iq).
- Interim exploration evidence committed by this issue's branch (append-only,
  real runs against real netlist shas): `sim/pvt/results/20260921T065703Z/`
  (the `RZ L=24µ` + poly-SEED interim sizing, superseded within the same
  issue by the frozen `RZ L=32µ` + nfet-SEED sizing) is kept for the record
  trail and is cited here so its numbers are not mistaken for final ones.
- The runtime-discipline stage (still undesigned, still the ratified path to
  USB compliance) is unchanged.
- If a later revision (the trim-switch fix, or a further pass) shows the
  post-trim rows can or cannot be met, that finding gets its own record —
  not a silent edit of this one.
