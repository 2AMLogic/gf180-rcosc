Method: single-corner transients run one at a time. Shares are in **period** (ns), the additive quantity: delay adds, frequency does not. Each item's contribution = period with the item present minus period with it removed. The telescoping table removes items in the order shown, so its rows sum to the total shift exactly; the leave-one-out table removes each item alone from the full netlist, and its sum differs from the total by the interaction term, reported as the residual.

Two substrate models are reported because the extractor ties the minted substrate node `vsubs` to ground only through a 1 Tohm shunt (`Rvsubs_dctie`, klt `extract_parasitics.py` issue #1263): **A** = netlist exactly as extracted (reproduces the 27-point record); **B** = same netlist with that shunt replaced by 1 mohm (a grounded p-substrate, as on silicon). The step between them is itself a row.

## Reading the table

* Model A reproduces the 27-point record exactly at these corners (`ff/-40/3.6` -36.86 %, `ss/-40/3.6` -18.78 %, `tt/27/3.3` -25.36 %).
* The device-geometry row is the parasitic-free extracted devices (layout AS/AD/PS/PD, drawn W/L) against the schematic devices, under the hard-tied substrate (model B); it is not zero and changes sign between corners, so it is reported, not folded into the parasitics.
* "Comparator input capacitance" is a device (gate + junction) quantity that exists identically in the schematic; it cannot be removed as a parasitic. Its layout-dependent part (junction areas/perimeters) is inside the device-geometry row; its routing part is inside the `vc` net-to-substrate row, because the extractor reports one lumped C per net and cannot split MiM bottom plate from routing.
